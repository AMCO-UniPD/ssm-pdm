# A Quantile Regression Approach for Remaining Useful Life Estimation with State Space Models

This is the official repository for the paper "A Quantile Regression Approach for Remaining Useful Life Estimation with State Space Models" by Frizzo et al, submitted to J3C 2025: IFAC Joint Conference on Computers, Cognition and Communication, to be held in Padova, Italy, 15-18 September 2025.

>[!info]
Paper Abstract
Predictive Maintenance (PdM) is pivotal in Industry 4.0 and 5.0, proactively enhancing efficiency through accurate equipment Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction,
thus optimizing maintenance scheduling and reducing unexpected failures and premature interventions.
This paper introduces a novel Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation approach leveraging State Space Models (SSM) for efficient long-term sequence modeling.
To handle model uncertainty, Simoultaneous Quantile Regression (SQR) is integrated into the SSM, enabling multiple quantile estimations.
The proposed method is benchmarked against traditional sequence modelling techniques (LSTM, Transformer, Informer)
using the C-MAPSS dataset. Results demonstrate superior accuracy and computational efficiency of SSM models,
underscoring their potential for high-stakes industrial applications.
