# Paper requirements to be accepted

Each reviewer will leave a final mark (in letters, from `D` (Unacceptable) to `A` (consider the paper for the best paper award)) and I should do a sort of average of these marks in order to make the final decision. In general the threshold to accept a paper should be `B/B-` (looking at the `Accept` or `Accept if possible` description of `B` and `B-` marks). However Gian said that in case the conference wants to reach a certain amount of paper the threshold can be lowered to `C/C+` → in any case this will be decided after all the reviews have arrived, so after the dealine of June 20th, 2025.

In particular the marks for the papers are the following:

| Mark | Meaning | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A (5.0) | Award Candidate | Definitely accept |
| B+ (4.5) | Journal material | Accept |
| B (4.0) | Solid conference paper | Accept |
| B- (3.5) | Fair conference paper | Accept |
| C (3.0) | Controversial/amendable | Accept with discretion |
| C- (2.5) | Marginal | Last resort |
| D (2.0) | Substandard | Unacceptable |
| U (1.0) | Unsuitable: previouslyu published, no audience, etc. | Reject with explanation |
